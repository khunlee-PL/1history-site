Germany · 962

The Holy Roman Empire

In 962 Otto I is crowned emperor by Pope John XII, an event conventionally treated as the beginning of the polity later called the Holy Roman Empire. The empire develops as a loose and changing federation of territories in Central Europe and survives until its dissolution in 1806.

Guatemala · 950

Classic Maya collapse

Paleoclimate records show repeated droughts during the centuries of political decline in the southern Maya lowlands. Water management likely affects local resilience, but city-by-city outcomes depend on politics, trade, environment and conflict as well as rainfall.

Mali · 1000

The Ghana Empire reaches the western Sahel

By the eleventh century the Ghana Empire controls much of the gold and salt trade of the western Sahel, and its network reaches the lands of present-day western Mali. Its merchants link the southern goldfields to the caravan routes of the Sahara.

Tuvalu · 900

The settlement of Tuvalu

Voyagers from Samoa and Tonga settle Tuvalu's low coral islands. They bring crops, animals, and navigation traditions, and each island preserves founding voyages through oral genealogy.

Armenia · 961

Ani, city of churches

The Bagratid kings make Ani their capital in 961. The city becomes a major medieval commercial and religious center, later remembered by the epithet 'the city of a thousand and one churches.'

And far away…

Türkiye · 961

Ani becomes a royal capital

The Bagratid king makes Ani his capital, and the city grows on the Akhurian gorge into a walled centre so filled with churches that it is remembered as a city of a thousand and one churches.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Kyrgyzstan · 960

The Kara-Khanids take Islam

The Kara-Khanid rulers of the Chuy and Kashgar regions adopt Islam, traditionally associated with Satuq Bughra Khan. The dynasty supports an emerging Islamic Turkic literary culture centered on Balasagun and Kashgar.
