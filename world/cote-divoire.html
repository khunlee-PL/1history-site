Côte d'Ivoire · 1710

The Kong Empire

Dyula Muslim traders build the Kong Empire at the crossroads of the gold, kola and salt routes — a merchant state of scholars and caravans that rules the north until Samori's armies burn it.

Barbados · 1660

Sugar plantations in the Caribbean

Barbadian sugar estates combine wind- or animal-powered mills with boiling houses where cane juice is processed through heated copper vessels. Enslaved workers face long hours, heat, machinery and other serious hazards throughout cane cultivation and sugar processing.

Bolivia · 1650

Potosí

Potosí becomes one of the Spanish Empire's great silver-mining centers. From 1573 the colonial mita requires more than 200 Indigenous communities to send roughly one-seventh of their adult men for rotating labor service. Mining, mercury exposure, coercion and disease impose severe human costs, but later claims of eight million deaths are not a reliable demographic estimate.

Bhutan · 1710

The tshechu

Tshechu festivals bring masked religious dances, music and communal gatherings to dzongs and monasteries across Bhutan. In Buddhist tradition, viewing the dances and large sacred images is considered spiritually meritorious, and the festivals remain major public events.

Costa Rica · 1709

Pablo Presbere leads the Talamanca revolt

In 1709 the Indigenous leader Pablo Presbere helps lead a major uprising against Spanish missions in Talamanca. Colonial forces eventually suppress the revolt and capture its leaders, but Spanish authority in the region remains limited for much of the colonial period.

And far away…

Georgia · 1709

Vakhtang VI's printing press

Vakhtang VI establishes a Georgian printing press in Tbilisi in 1709, printing religious works and later Rustaveli's epic. His court also sponsors legal codification, scholarship and historical writing.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Haiti · 1711

Cap-Français

Cap-Français rises on the north coast as the chief city of French Saint-Domingue, a hub of trade, administration and plantation wealth. Its theatres and townhouses earn it comparison with European cities before war transforms the colony.
