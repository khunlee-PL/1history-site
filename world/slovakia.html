Slovakia · 1735

Samuel Mikovíni maps the kingdom

The polymath Samuel Mikovíni teaches at the mining school of Banská Štiavnica and surveys the Kingdom of Hungary, drawing some of its first accurate maps.

Meanwhile, elsewhere…

Saint Lucia · 1765

Sugar and the enslaved majority

Planters carve the valleys into sugar estates worked by enslaved Africans, shipped in through the Middle Passage until they vastly outnumber the whites. Cane, rum and molasses make a few families rich. The labor that makes them rich is coerced, brutal and lethal, and the island's people are overwhelmingly the descendants of the enslaved.

At the same time…

Bolivia · 1650

Potosí

Potosí becomes one of the Spanish Empire's great silver-mining centers. From 1573 the colonial mita requires more than 200 Indigenous communities to send roughly one-seventh of their adult men for rotating labor service. Mining, mercury exposure, coercion and disease impose severe human costs, but later claims of eight million deaths are not a reliable demographic estimate.

Across the world…

Mauritius · 1735

Bertrand-François Mahé de La Bourdonnais

The energetic governor Mahe de La Bourdonnais develops Bourbon and neighboring Ile de France. He builds ports and roads, binding the islands into a strategic Indian Ocean base.

In the same age…

Antigua and Barbuda · 1736

Prince Klaas

In 1736 colonial authorities accuse an enslaved man known as Prince Klaas, or Court, and others of plotting an uprising against Antigua's planter government. The authorities respond with mass trials, executions and deportations. Historians continue to debate how far the alleged conspiracy matched the plan described by colonial prosecutors.

And far away…

Ecuador · 1736

Measuring the shape of the Earth

A French Academy of Sciences expedition led by Charles Marie de La Condamine measures an arc of meridian near the equator to help determine the figure of the Earth.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Iran · 1736

Nader Shah

Nader Shah rises from military command to the Iranian throne in 1736, defeats Afghan and Ottoman forces and builds a short-lived empire stretching into the Caucasus, Central Asia and India.
