Croatia · 1069

The kingdom of Peter Krešimir IV

Under King Peter Krešimir IV the Croatian kingdom reaches a wide extent, and royal charters describe the Adriatic as the king's own sea. Documents are issued from coastal towns such as Biograd.

Mali · 1000

The Ghana Empire reaches the western Sahel

By the eleventh century the Ghana Empire controls much of the gold and salt trade of the western Sahel, and its network reaches the lands of present-day western Mali. Its merchants link the southern goldfields to the caravan routes of the Sahara.

Kyrgyzstan · 1070

Kutadgu Bilig

Yusuf of Balasagun finishes the Kutadgu Bilig, the Wisdom of Royal Glory, and presents it to his Kara-Khanid prince. In some 6,500 couplets four characters, standing for Justice, Fortune, Intellect and Contentment, argue how a ruler should live. It is the earliest surviving major work of Islamic Turkic literature, written here in the Chuy.

Sri Lanka · 1070

Vijayabahu I restores the kingdom

After a period of Chola rule from southern India, Vijayabahu I drives out the occupiers and is crowned, making Polonnaruwa his capital. The island's Sinhalese kingship is re-established.

Tajikistan · 1070

Nasir Khusraw in Badakhshan

Poet, philosopher and Ismaili missionary, Nasir Khusraw records a seven-year journey to Mecca and Cairo in his Safarnama, then, hounded as a heretic, ends his days in the Pamir valleys. The mountain Ismailis of Badakhshan still revere him as the founder of their faith.

Belarus · 1067

First mention of Minsk

Vseslav of Polotsk clashes with the sons of Yaroslav on the frozen Nemiga river. The chronicle records the slaughter and, in doing so, names Menesk for the first time — the earliest written mention of the town that becomes Minsk, the future capital.

Chad · 1075

Hummay brings Islam to Kanem

The mai Hummay, first Muslim king of the Sayfawa line, adopts Islam and ties his court at Njimi to the wider Muslim world. Arabic letters, law and scholars travel the trade roads into Kanem; its dynasty later keeps a hostel in Cairo for pilgrims on the long road to Mecca.
