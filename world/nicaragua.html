Nicaragua · 1687

The Miskito Kingdom

The Miskito Kingdom develops along the Caribbean coast with British military and commercial support. Its autonomy limits Spanish control and later complicates Nicaragua's attempts to incorporate the region.

Barbados · 1660

Sugar plantations in the Caribbean

Barbadian sugar estates combine wind- or animal-powered mills with boiling houses where cane juice is processed through heated copper vessels. Enslaved workers face long hours, heat, machinery and other serious hazards throughout cane cultivation and sugar processing.

Bolivia · 1650

Potosí

Potosí becomes one of the Spanish Empire's great silver-mining centers. From 1573 the colonial mita requires more than 200 Indigenous communities to send roughly one-seventh of their adult men for rotating labor service. Mining, mercury exposure, coercion and disease impose severe human costs, but later claims of eight million deaths are not a reliable demographic estimate.

Mali · 1600

The Azalai salt caravan crosses the Sahara

The Azalai salt caravan sets out from Timbuktu across the Sahara to the mines of Taoudenni and back. Long files of camels carry grain and goods north and return laden with slabs of salt.

Côte d'Ivoire · 1687

Aniaba sails to the court of France

Aniaba, a young man from the Assinie coast, is sent to the court of Louis XIV, where he is baptized and educated before returning home. His voyage marks one of the earliest direct links between the shore and France.

Guinea-Bissau · 1687

Bissau

The Portuguese plant a fortified post at Bissau, on land of the Papel, whose kings tax and tolerate them. For two centuries it stays a cramped garrison hemmed to its walls, dependent on African rulers for food, water and the very captives it ships.

Honduras · 1687

The Miskito kings

Miskito leaders develop a monarchy and political hierarchy that interacts closely with British officials and merchants. British recognition, weapons and trade strengthen Miskito power, while local leaders use those ties to pursue their own regional interests.
