Niger · 1449

The Sultanate of Aïr

Tuareg nomads found the sultanate of Aïr around the caravan city of Agadez — a desert crossroads where the trans-Saharan trade in salt, gold and slaves meets the Sahel, its mud minaret a landmark for centuries.

Syria · 1400

Timur sacks Damascus

Timur captures and sacks Damascus in 1401 after defeating Mamluk forces. Contemporary accounts describe extensive destruction and the forced transfer of skilled artisans to Samarkand; the city later rebuilds under Mamluk rule.

Venezuela · 1498

Columbus reaches the South American mainland

On his third voyage, Christopher Columbus reaches the Gulf of Paria and the Orinoco delta and steps ashore, his first landing on the South American mainland.

Mozambique · 1505

Portugal establishes rule on the Mozambique coast

Portugal establishes fortified positions on the Mozambique coast from the early sixteenth century and gradually expands influence along the Zambezi. The prazo estate system develops later in the interior, combining land grants, trade, armed power and coerced labor.

In the same age…

Bhutan · 1450

Pema Lingpa the terton

The treasure-revealer Pema Lingpa dives into a Bumthang lake holding a lit butter lamp and surfaces with it still burning and a hidden scripture in hand. The pool is named Mebartsho, the Burning Lake. His sacred dances and metalwork endure, and the royal family traces its blood back to him.

And far away…

Bosnia and Herzegovina · 1448

Herzegovina, the duke's land

Grand Duke Stjepan Vukčić Kosača, ruling the south from Blagaj, styles himself 'herceg' — duke — of Saint Sava. His title outlives his state: the Ottomans name the land they take from him the Sanjak of Herzegovina, and 'the duke's land' is what the region is still called.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Dominica · 1450

The Kalinago stronghold

Kalinago communities use Dominica's rugged terrain and eastern coastline as part of a wider Lesser Antillean network of settlement, exchange and warfare. Strong resistance and difficult terrain help preserve a substantial Indigenous presence on Dominica longer than on many neighboring islands.
