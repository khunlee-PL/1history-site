Madagascar · 1817

Radama I opens the kingdom to Europe

Radama I opens the Merina kingdom to European contact, welcoming missionaries and advisers who introduce a Latin alphabet for Malagasy and new technologies.

Meanwhile, elsewhere…

Argentina · 1817

San Martín crosses the Andes

In 1817 José de San Martín leads the Army of the Andes from western Argentina across high mountain passes into Chile. The campaign helps defeat royalist forces there and becomes a decisive stage in the independence struggles of Chile and later Peru.

Marshall Islands · 1817

Kotzebue charts the Ratak

The Russian brig Rurik under Otto von Kotzebue works north through the eastern chain, mapping atoll after atoll and recording the name Ratak for it. The artist Louis Choris paints the people and their canoes from life. Kotzebue trades iron and befriends the chief Kadu, who sails on with him, giving Europe its first close account of Marshallese seafaring.

Across the world…

Sri Lanka · 1817

The Uva rebellion

Two years after the Kandyan kingdom's fall, a revolt breaks out in the Uva region against British rule. Its suppression brings heavy destruction to the highland districts.

In the same age…

Barbados · 1816

Bussa's Rebellion

In April 1816 thousands of enslaved people in Barbados rise in the largest slave revolt in the island's history. Colonial militia and troops suppress the rebellion, killing many participants and executing or deporting others; Bussa is remembered as one of its principal leaders.

And far away…

Benin · 1818

Francisco Félix de Sousa

Francisco Félix de Sousa, a Brazilian-born merchant based at Ouidah, becomes one of the coast's most influential slave traders in the early nineteenth century. He supports Ghezo's rise to power and receives the title Chacha, linking Atlantic commerce closely to Dahomean politics.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Chile · 1818

Battle of Maipú

The patriot victory at Maipú in April 1818 secures the independence movement's control of central Chile after José de San Martín's Army of the Andes had crossed into Chile the previous year. The campaign then helps open the way for further operations against Spanish rule in Peru.
