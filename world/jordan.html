Jordan · 106

The province of Arabia Petraea

Rome annexes the Nabataean realm and organises it as the province of Arabia Petraea, with Petra and later Bostra among its chief cities. Roman roads and garrisons tie the region to the empire.

Pakistan · 150

Greco-Buddhist art

Gandharan artists develop influential anthropomorphic images of the Buddha using artistic conventions shaped by South Asian, Iranian and Hellenistic traditions. Gandharan styles spread widely through Buddhist networks, alongside other important image-making centers such as Mathura.

Saint Lucia · 200

First settlers reach Iouanalao

Archaeological sites on Saint Lucia show settlement by ceramic-making communities during the first millennium CE. These communities farm cassava and other crops, fish coastal waters and participate in the wider exchange networks of the Lesser Antilles.

Libya · 110

Leptis Magna

Leptis Magna grows from a Phoenician-Punic port into one of Roman Africa's great cities, with monumental forums, baths, a theater and harbor. Its ruins survive on an exceptional scale along the Libyan coast.

Belize · 100

Lamanai

Lamanai, beside the New River Lagoon, is occupied for roughly three millennia and remains important into the Spanish colonial period. Its temples, later churches and nearby industrial remains record repeated transformations rather than a single moment of abandonment.

Florida · 100

Apalachee farmers

In the fertile north, the Apalachee build mound towns and farm corn, part of the Mississippian world stretching across the Southeast.

Uzbekistan · 100

Buddhism at Termez

At Termez on the Amu Darya, monasteries such as Fayaz Tepe gather monks, stupas and schools of sculpture. The town becomes a northern waypoint for Buddhism as it travels along the trade routes.
