Djibouti · 1285

The Ifat Sultanate and Zeila

The medieval Ifat Sultanate, ruled by the Walashma dynasty, controls trade routes linking the port of Zeila with the Ethiopian highlands and the interior. Repeated wars with Christian Ethiopian rulers weaken Ifat, and related Walashma rulers later established the Adal Sultanate.

Antigua and Barbuda · 1300

Island Caribs

By the late pre-Columbian period, Kalinago-speaking communities are established across much of the Lesser Antilles, including Antigua. European accounts later emphasized warfare and raiding, but archaeology and ethnohistory also show regional travel, exchange, fishing and settlement.

Mauritania · 1300

Salt from the sebkha

Twice a year the camel caravans, the azalai, cross to the salt pans of Idjil, cutting bars of gray rock salt to trade south for millet and gold. Where the Sahel has no salt, a slab can weigh nearly against its own weight in gold.

Senegal · 1287

The kingdom of Waalo

In the delta where the Senegal River meets the Atlantic, the Wolof kingdom of Waalo takes shape under its ruler, the brak. Its position at the river's mouth makes it an early meeting point for trans-Saharan and, later, Atlantic trade.

Mongolia · 1279

The Mongol Empire at its greatest extent

From Korea to Poland, and from Siberia to the Persian Gulf. Sixteen percent of the land surface of the planet, held by a people who at the outset numbered perhaps a million.

And far away…

Andorra · 1278

Paréage of Andorra

To end a war of raids between the Bishop of Urgell and the Count of Foix, arbitrators split the valleys between them: neither wins, both rule. The paréage makes Andorra a shared lordship under two princes at once — the institutional origin of the co-principality; under the 1993 constitution, the Bishop of Urgell and the President of France remain co-princes with limited constitutional roles.

Elsewhere on the globe…

Iran · 1295

The Ilkhanate becomes Muslim

Ilkhan Ghazan converts to Islam in 1295 and makes it central to his rule. His conversion accelerates the integration of the Mongol Ilkhanate into the Islamic political and cultural world of Iran.
