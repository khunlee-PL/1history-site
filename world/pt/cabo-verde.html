Cabo Verde · 1462

Ribeira Grande is founded

Portuguese settlers establish Ribeira Grande on Santiago in the 1460s. The town becomes an important early Atlantic colonial port, closely tied to commerce with West Africa and to the trafficking and forced labor of enslaved Africans.

Venezuela · 1498

Columbus reaches the South American mainland

On his third voyage, Christopher Columbus reaches the Gulf of Paria and the Orinoco delta and steps ashore, his first landing on the South American mainland.

Ao mesmo tempo…

Mozambique · 1505

Portugal estabelece o domínio na costa de Moçambique

Portugal estabelece posições fortificadas na costa de Moçambique a partir do início do século XVI e expande gradualmente a influência ao longo do Zambeze. O sistema de prazos desenvolve-se mais tarde no interior, combinando concessões de terra, comércio, poder armado e trabalho forçado.

Por todo o mundo…

Niger · 1400

The azalai crosses the Ténéré

Great camel caravans, the azalai, cross the Ténéré between Agadez and the salt pans of Bilma. They carry salt, dates and millet along one of the Sahara's hardest routes.

Na mesma época…

Syria · 1400

Timur sacks Damascus

Timur captures and sacks Damascus in 1401 after defeating Mamluk forces. Contemporary accounts describe extensive destruction and the forced transfer of skilled artisans to Samarkand; the city later rebuilds under Mamluk rule.

E ao longe…

Sierra Leone · 1462

Pedro de Sintra names Sierra Leone

The Portuguese captain Pedro de Sintra sails past a range of thunder-wreathed peaks above a great harbor and names them Serra Lyoa, the Lion Mountains. Whether for their shape or the growl of storms breaking on them, the name sticks, and becomes a country.

Noutro ponto do globo…

Bosnia and Herzegovina · 1463

The kingdom falls to the Ottomans

Mehmed the Conqueror storms into Bosnia and takes fortress after fortress. The last king, Stephen Tomašević, surrenders at Ključ on a promise of safe conduct; the sultan's jurist voids it, and Stephen is beheaded. In one summer the medieval kingdom ends and Ottoman Bosnia begins.
