Privacy Policy — 1History

21 August 2026

1History collects no personal data. None.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours. It works entirely offline; the only network activity is Apple's App Store system delivering the app's own content package when you request it. Everything you do in the app — reading, settings, personal markers — stays on your device.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or delete.

The app's bundle carries a privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declaring exactly this: no tracking, no tracking domains, no collected data.
