1History — Our Shared Past on One Globe

Ever wondered what the rest of the world was like the year you were born?
Or why your “World History” course was 90% Western history?

No memorization. No book skimming. Play the guess-the-year game solo or with up to ten players. 1History takes you on a tour through world history — from the Big Bang to COVID-19 — as you have never experienced it before.

Tap any dot on the globe and read what happened there: a few honest sentences from the historical record, with no sermon attached. Great Zimbabwe and the Magna Carta sit at the same size.

700+ events across the globe, with pictures — and counting. More packs and regions on the way.

No account. No tracking. No server.

The entire history is in the app, on your device — pictures included. It works completely offline.
